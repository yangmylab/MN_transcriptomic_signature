## 2. Differential expression is the wrong instrument (E2)

- **584** glomerular MN-vs-control meta-DEGs (353 up / 231 down), median *I*² = 0 — the array and
  RNA-seq cohorts agree.
- **55.3 %** replicate in ERCB (252/456, same sign); only **32.4 %** in the tubulointerstitium.
- Against other glomerulopathies thresholded signal is thin: **18** meta-DEGs vs FSGS, **60** vs MCD,
  **16** vs other GN.
- Yet a multivariate classifier reaches **nested-CV AUC 0.911–0.926** and **external ERCB AUC
  0.861–0.922** (0.922 at the full feature set) with permutation *p* = 0.048.

**Reading:** discrimination lives in many genes of moderate effect. Negative "MN vs FSGS DEG" studies
should not be read as evidence that the diseases are transcriptomically indistinguishable.

## 3. The signal is compartment-resolved and modular (E2)

- Glomerular network: 7,882 genes, soft power 18, **9 modules**; tubulointerstitial: 7,616 genes,
  power 10, 7 modules.
- MN association survives adjustment for podocyte/fibroblast/myeloid composition in **two** modules:
  **pink** (84 genes, cell-surface / calcium-binding; adjusted *p* = 1.0 × 10⁻⁵) and **red**
  (144 genes, lysosomal / glycosylation; adjusted *p* = 6.6 × 10⁻⁴). A third module (black, ECM +
  EMT) loses significance once composition is adjusted.
- **All 9 glomerular and all 7 TI modules are preserved in ERCB** (median Z 29.0 / 26.6); grey and
  gold fail, so the test discriminates rather than rubber-stamps.
- Co-expression **axes** are highly reproducible (eigengene bicor 0.92–0.96) while gene
  **membership** is only partly so (Jaccard ≈ 0.3) — which is why hubs were admitted only after
  independent preservation.
- **No TI module tracks MN at all** — consistent with the compartment-specificity result.

## 4. The classifier, and exactly how far it goes (E2, with named limits)

| Cohort | n (MN/other) | AUC (95 % CI) |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery (apparent) | 147 (51/96) | 0.922 (0.878–0.967) |
| **ERCB external** | 170 (21/149) | **0.816 (0.721–0.910)** |
| **RNA-seq independent** | 273 (62/211) | **0.901 (0.862–0.939)** |
| Laser-capture (LCM) | 26 (12/14) | 0.929 (0.824–1.000) |
| ERCB, other CDF revision | 187 (21/166) | 0.848 (0.759–0.938) |
| *Reference: 13,379-gene elastic net* | 170 (21/149) | *0.922 (0.862–0.982)* |

Panel: **SLAMF8, TRPC6, MYOZ2, PPP1R3C** — all four coefficients positive (OR 2.59–3.40).

What it is **not**:

- **Not equal to the whole-transcriptome model.** After the platform-harmonisation correction (F62)
  the 19k-gene elastic net reaches 0.922 in ERCB versus the panel's 0.816. The honest claim is
  *interpretability and transportability*, not superiority.
- **Not validated at the strict null.** ERCB label-permutation *p* = 0.005, but the stricter
  refit-on-shuffled-labels null gives ***p* = 0.11 — not significant**, and the manuscript says so.
- **Not calibrated.** Calibration-in-the-large +0.86 in ERCB, −0.21 in RNA-seq; slopes 6–45. A
  two-parameter per-platform recalibration brings it to −0.003 and cuts the Brier score from 0.845 to
  0.096 without touching AUC.
- **Not compartment-general.** It falls to chance in the tubulointerstitium of the same patients
  (0.449 / 0.539) — the strongest specificity result in the paper.
- **Not protein-confirmed.** That is the open E4 step.

## 5. What the atlas adds (E1)

- 49 samples → 432,378 cells → **236,107-cell atlas, 16 cell types**; 1,328 bulk samples deconvolved.
- Glomerular MN is **podocyte-rich relative to other nephropathies** (0.637 vs 0.583, adjusted
  *p* = 3.3 × 10⁻⁴) but **not** relative to healthy glomeruli (0.637 vs 0.615, adjusted *p* = 0.29).
  The claim is therefore "podocyte-rich relative to other nephropathies", never "podocyte loss".
- Deconvolution is fragile in detail: filtered and all-gene signatures disagree on the dominant cell
  type in 1,314/1,328 samples. Only the podocyte result is interpreted.
- Panel-gene pseudobulk yields no BH-surviving comparison (15 vs 10 donors) — hypothesis-grade.

## 6. Negative results, reported as findings

1. **Subtyping does not reproduce.** Stable *k* = 2 in the array cohort (PAC 0.0015), no stable
   partition in RNA-seq (PAC 0.215), subtype genes Jaccard 0.007, and the array split tracks technical
   covariates (median sample correlation 0.686 vs 0.638, *p* = 4 × 10⁻⁴).
2. **Tubulointerstitial DE replicates poorly** (32 %) and is null against FSGS in every cohort.
3. **The strict permutation null for the panel fails** (*p* = 0.11).

## 7. Methodological finding worth carrying beyond this paper (E2)

"Use training statistics" is **not** a cross-platform transfer. Removing the transductive z-scoring
was necessary but left discovery μ/σ applied to raw HG-U133 values: the median ERCB gene sat at
|z| ≈ 4 instead of ≈ 0.6, the linear predictor saturated, and four comparator diagnoses ranked
*above* MN (RPGN 0.05, FSGS/MCD 0.01–0.14). Rank-preserving quantile mapping onto the discovery
reference — label-blind — restores a valid transfer (0.861–0.922), and the panel's answer is
unchanged by the same treatment (0.816 → 0.817, calibration improved). Any marker paper claiming
external validation should state both steps.

## 8. What is still missing, and why it is not a data-availability problem

| Gap | Blocker | Route |
|---|---|---|
| Clinical outcomes (eGFR, UPCR, remission) → prognostic model, subtype–outcome | access-controlled, not absent | NIDDK Central Repository R4R, study DOI `10.58020/f2ae-n094`; packet ready in `Manuscript/DATA_REQUESTS.md` |
| Participant map for the RNA-seq cohorts | held by submitters | same request |
| Protein-level validation of the four panel proteins | wet lab | pre-specified plan in `Manuscript/IF_validation_plan.md`; MYOZ2 first |

No new public dataset would change any claim in the paper. Downloading more GEO series would add
samples, not the missing *variables* — and would risk re-importing the redundancy this study spent
its first phase removing.
